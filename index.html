Routes of the Week

✈️

Weekly Featured Route

Every week, a special route will be selected based on an airport experiencing high traffic on that specific day.

Who can fly?

Any pilot can fly the Route of the Week, regardless of rank.

Landing Requirement

The flight must land on the same day as the Route of the Week announcement.

Multiplier

If the route is already unlocked for your rank, you receive a 1.5× flight-time multiplier.

If the route is not unlocked, you may still fly it, but without the multiplier.

Base of the Week

🏢

Weekly Special Base

Every week, one airport will be selected as the Base of the Week.

Example

VABB — Mumbai

2× Multiplier

Every eligible SpiceJet flight departing from or arriving at the selected base receives a 2× flight-time multiplier.

Important

Only SpiceJet flights are eligible for the Base of the Week multiplier.

The special base changes every week.

Events

🔥

Weekly Group Flights

All pilots are expected to participate in our weekly events. These are massive group flights designed to bring the entire virtual together.

Event Multiplier

Special events may feature a high flight-time multiplier. Check the event announcement for the multiplier and rules.

Event Leadership

Always follow the instructions of the Event Manager / Event Lead.

Departure Order

The CEO should always be the #1 pilot to depart during group events.

Fly Together

Try your best to fly and land together with the group.

PIREP

📋

Flight Reports

PIREP stands for Pilot Report. A PIREP is the flight report submitted after completing your flight.

File Only After Completion

Submit your PIREP only after completing the entire flight.

Make Sure Everything Is Correct

Your PIREP should contain the correct:

  • Flight number
  • Flight time
  • Departure airport
  • Arrival airport
PIREP Not Approved?

If your PIREP is genuine but remains unapproved, open a support ticket in the server for assistance.
